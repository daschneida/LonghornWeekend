The weekend at a glance

Solid line is on foot. The whole campus walk fits between 1:40 and 3:10, and the dotted spur is the shortcut if Brenham runs long.

Saturday

Barbecue in Brenham, 90 minutes on campus, then the Alumni Center as a base from 5:10. Everything after 3:10 belongs to the team. Forecast: 95°F, 20% chance of rain.

  1. 9:00 AM

    Out of Houston, US-290 west

    Take 290, not I-10. The interstate routes you through Columbus onto 71 and the food along there is thin. 290 is the barbecue corridor.

  2. 10:20 AM

    Truth BBQ, Brenham

    2990 US-290 · opens 11:00 · map

    Be in line well before the doors open — at 10:20 you are near the front, which matters more than the fifteen minutes it cost to leave early. The wait runs 30 to 40 minutes and they hand out cold beer while you stand in it. Shaded picnic tables outside, brisket and pork ribs are the order.

    Brenham is roughly the midpoint, which is why this works. Anything further west and you don't make the parade.

  3. 11:50 AM

    Back on the road

    The tripwire

    Brenham to Austin is ninety minutes. If you are not pulling out of that lot by 12:05, cut the Speedway Mall from the campus walk — go straight east on 21st from Jester to San Jacinto instead of north to Dean Keeton and back down.

    That saves fifteen minutes and is the only cut that doesn't cost much. Decide it here, in the car. Not on campus at five past three.

  4. 1:20 PM

    Austin — bags in, car valeted

    Hyatt House Austin/Downtown · 901 Neches St

    Drop the luggage at the front desk and put the car in the garage. You do not touch it again until Sunday morning — campus towing runs from midnight Friday, nearby garages are $25 to $75 today, and Trinity goes one-way southbound an hour after kickoff.

    Valet it, don't self-park. Hunting a garage spot with three people and a loaded car is the twenty-minute version; handing over the keys is the twelve-minute version. Then a rideshare to 21st and Guadalupe, eight minutes.

  5. 1:40 PM

    Onto the West Mall

    Out of the car and straight in. No Co-op today — that moved to Sunday. The West Mall runs level and dead east to the Tower, so it's in front of you the whole way — the best arrival on campus and the one most visitors never take.

    At the base, look straight up. Three hundred and seven feet reads completely differently from underneath than it does in a photograph. Step into the Main Building lobby for the vaulted ceiling and the cool air.

    Observation deck tours are currently suspended.

  6. 2:00 PM

    South Mall and Littlefield Fountain

    Walk down the mall with the Six Pack flanking you — Parlin, Calhoun and Rainey on one side, Batts, Benedict and Mezes on the other. At the bottom, turn around. That's the photograph: the 1933 fountain with the bronze horses coming out of the water, the ship's prow, and the Tower standing behind it at the top of the rise.

    Twenty minutes, and it's worth every one of them.

  7. 2:20 PM

    Jester Center

    201 E 21st St · straight east on 21st

    Ten minutes. Point at a window and tell him which one was yours.

  8. 2:33 PM

    Gregory Gym

    Across Speedway from Jester. The 1930 building with the arcaded front is still the original — everything behind it got added later.

  9. 2:40 PM

    North on the Speedway Mall

    This isn't the Speedway you remember

    It was a service road when you lived in Jester — delivery trucks, cars, students dodging both. Between 2016 and 2018 UT spent $36 million turning it into a thirty-six-foot pedestrian corridor running exactly this line, Jester Circle up to Dean Keeton. Golden sand-molded brick laid in herringbone, and they engineered the soil under the paving specifically to keep the mature live oaks alive. Which is why this half mile has shade when the rest of campus doesn't.

    North from Gregory you pass the PCL, then Welch, and come out at Dean Keeton between the physics, math and astronomy building on one side and the Cockrell School's engineering complex on the other.

    Your route ends at the engineering corner of campus by accident. Tell him that's not an accident of interest — the university's center of gravity moved east off the Tower decades ago, and this corridor is what that looks like built.

  10. 2:55 PM

    East on Dean Keeton to San Jacinto

    Six minutes, and the burnt orange thickens the whole way. Leave the Speedway Mall on time — this is the one hard deadline of the day.

  11. 3:05 PM

    Onto Bevo Blvd, heading south

    Dean Keeton puts you in at the north end, so you'll pass Smokey's Midway on DeLoss Dodds Way first. Work south along San Jacinto toward Gate 1, where the crowd gathers for Bevo's arrival. It's eight to ten minutes through thick people — and it's the right direction anyway, since Big Bertha's Bazaar is down at that end.

    Note where the Alumni Center is as you pass it — 2110 San Jacinto, the building that anchors the north end of Bevo Blvd. That's your air conditioning for the rest of the afternoon. It opens at 4:00.

    If the crowd is impossible, stop where you are. The parade comes up San Jacinto; any spot on the rail works.

  12. 3:30 PM

    Bevo Parade

    San Jacinto Blvd, near Gate 1

    Be in place by 3:10 for a decent spot along the rail. Bevo arrives with the cheerleaders and members of the band. He is a genuinely enormous animal and the photographs never quite convey it.

    Watch who's holding the halter

    The men in the cowboy hats walking Bevo down San Jacinto are the Silver Spurs — an honorary service organization founded at UT in 1937 and responsible for transporting and handling the mascot since 1945. There are about a hundred members. Four are trained handlers, and those four are the ones you'll see on the rail and later on the field.

    This is Bevo XV. He's been on the job since 2016, makes something like fifty appearances a year, and lives on a ranch north of Austin the rest of the time. The Spurs' rule at the rail is the simple one: look, don't touch. Those horns clear five feet and they were self-defense equipment long before they were a logo.

  13. 4:15 PM

    Stadium Stampede

    The team walks in through the crowd, roughly 45 minutes after the parade. Same stretch of San Jacinto — stay put and you'll catch both.

    If he needs air conditioning before 4:00: the Frank Denius Family Athletics Hall of Fame is indoors and free to everyone, entered through Smokey's Midway on the north side of the stadium. No membership, no ticket.

  14. 4:35 PM

    Big Bertha's Bazaar

    Moody Center plaza

    This one's for the band kid

    Big Bertha lives here before the march — the Longhorn Band's bass drum, around eight feet across, old enough to have its own history and heavy enough to need a crew to move it. There's an official photo op with her on the plaza.

    Get the picture now. In ninety minutes she'll be at the front of the march and surrounded four deep.

  15. 5:10 PM

    Dinner inside, at the Alumni Center

    Etter-Harbin Alumni Center · 2110 San Jacinto · ten-minute walk north up Bevo Blvd

    Grandpa's Life Membership covers all three of you

    Members bring one guest free, plus anyone 18 and under. He signs you and your son in on his card. Life Members have historically had early access before annual members are let in — worth a call to Texas Exes to confirm it still holds.

    Inside: air conditioning, the only full-service bars on Bevo Blvd, food, live music, and clean indoor restrooms. Roughly 10,000 people pass through per game, so it's busy — but it's seated, and it's cold.

    Bring a card and cash. Sources disagree on whether the bars are cashless this season.

    Eat here rather than standing at the food trucks in the heat. The wristband is come-and-go until kickoff, so you are not spending it all in one sitting.

  16. 5:30 PM

    Step outside for the band

    The Alumni Center fronts San Jacinto, which is the street the Longhorn Band marches down. Walk out to the patio, watch it pass with Big Bertha at the front, and walk back in.

    If he plays what they're playing, he'll recognize Texas Fight and March Grandioso before they're halfway past.

    Confirm the march route on the gameday map Thursday — if it doesn't come up San Jacinto, walk out to the stadium side and back.

  17. 5:45 PM

    Back inside, second cooldown

    Twenty minutes in the A/C with a restroom stop before three and a half hours in a stadium seat. This is the one that matters — take it even if nobody feels like they need it yet.

    The halftime trap

    Texas Exes advertises that you're welcome back at halftime. You are not, if you're at the game. DKR has no re-entry — once you scan in, leaving ends your night. That perk is for people watching on the TVs here.

    Don't let him plan a halftime cooldown. The 5:45 break is the last one.

  18. 6:05 PM

    Out and over to your gate

    Ten minutes from the Alumni Center to the stadium, longer as the crowd thickens. Go now rather than at 6:15.

  19. 6:15 PM

    Into the stadium

    Enter at the gate printed on your ticket. No re-entry — once you're through, you're in for the night.

  20. 6:40 PM

    In your seats for the pregame show

    The band takes the field about twenty minutes out and forms the Texas logo. For a kid who marches, this is the part worth being early for — more than kickoff itself.

  21. 7:00 PM

    Kickoff

    Texas comes in off a 24–23 comeback win over No. 1 Ohio State the week before. This place will be loud.

  22. 10:30 PM

    Stay to the end

    Win or lose, nobody leaves before the team faces the student section and everyone sings The Eyes of Texas with horns up. If Texas wins, the Tower turns burnt orange.

  23. 10:45 PM

    Walk home, or one more stop

    Kerbey Lane Cafe · 2606 Guadalupe St · open till midnight

    A mile and a half back to the hotel, downhill, the whole crowd moving your direction and the Tower lit behind you. Or detour to Kerbey Lane for queso and pancakes at eleven o'clock surrounded by students — which is a college town at full volume, and exactly what a fourteen-year-old should see.

Before you leave the hotel

The five things that actually go wrong.

  • Clear bag rule, strictly enforcedNon-clear bags can't exceed 4.5″ × 6.5″. Clear bags can't exceed 12″ × 6″ × 12″. There is no bag check at the stadium — wrong bag means walking it back.
  • Assume no outside drinksSealed and empty bottles are both on the prohibited list. Hydrate hard before the gate and buy inside.
  • No re-entry, and it overrides the Alumni CenterEverything outside has to happen before you scan in. The Texas Exes halftime-return perk does not apply to anyone sitting in the stadium.
  • Dress for 95°FHats and sunscreen. Still high 80s at kickoff, low 80s by the fourth quarter. You're outside and walking from 1:05 to 6:15. The Main Building lobby and the live oaks over the Speedway Mall are the only real shade on the route — and buy water on the Drag, because campus buildings are locked on a game Saturday.
  • Confirm the pregame times ThursdayParade, Stampede and band march times post at texaslonghorns.com/gameday during the week and can shift by fifteen or twenty minutes.

Three things to watch for

Point these out before they happen.

Smokey the Cannon
Fires after every Texas score. Much louder than he expects.
Big Bertha
He'll have already met her at the Bazaar. Watch for her leading the band out onto the field.
The Eyes of Texas
Sung at the end with the hand sign raised. Everyone stays. Nobody walks out.

Sunday

Sleep in. Shop without thinking about bag rules. Home by mid-afternoon.

  1. 9:30 AM

    Load the car and check out

    No alarm. Nothing this morning starts before ten.

  2. 10:00 AM

    University Co-op

    2246 Guadalupe St · opens 10:00 · map

    The whole reason it moved here: no clear-bag rule, no gate, no carrying it around for nine hours. Buy the sweatshirt and the mug. Three floors, and there's a discount section on the top one.

  3. 11:00 AM

    US-290 east

    Straight shot home, about two and a half hours. If anyone's hungry on the way out, Southside Market in Elgin is 25 miles down the road — founded 1882, the oldest barbecue joint in Texas, and the reason Elgin is the sausage capital of the state. Ask for the original all-beef hot guts.

The Capitol walkthrough is still on the site at capitol.html if you end up with a spare morning. Details verified the week of September 15, 2026. Pregame schedules and the Longhorn City Limits lineup are confirmed at texaslonghorns.com/gameday a few days out — worth a last check Thursday night.
